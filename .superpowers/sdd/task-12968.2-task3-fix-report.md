## Settled Boundary

- Query names are unique, route-allowed, order-preserving, and strictly percent-encoded under the public request-target ceiling; malformed Unicode and oversized targets become context-free `request_rejected` errors before I/O.
- A second validated binding snapshot is required after the synchronous policy callback. Trace data and trusted response limits come from private pre-dispatch snapshots, not live route/intent/request objects after the await.
- Injected hop responses must match the public primitive's bounded shape: final status `200..599`, canonical unique public address evidence, bounded Latin-1 response fields, conservative raw-header byte evidence, and route-owned body/wire ceilings.
- `Location` and `Retry-After` are singleton, bounded visible-ASCII control fields exposed as immutable optional values. They are never followed, parsed, slept on, or copied into trace/errors by the gateway.
- Only literal boolean `True` survives as retryable hop-error metadata. Unexpected or hostile provider state maps to the stable nonretryable `hop_failed` error, while cancellation still propagates.
