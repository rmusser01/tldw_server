## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The v1 import journal records a new pack and slot checkpoint before all item and generated-file writes. A process stop during writes can leave partial state. The completed-receipt replay handles jobs that committed their journal. Earlier interruption requires durable per-write ownership and recovery before retry or cleanup.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
