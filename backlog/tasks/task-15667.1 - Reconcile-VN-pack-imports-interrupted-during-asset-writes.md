## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The v1 import journal records a new pack and slot checkpoint before all item and generated-file writes. A process stop during writes can leave partial state. The completed-receipt replay handles jobs that committed their journal. Earlier interruption requires durable per-write ownership and recovery before retry or cleanup.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES:BEGIN -->
Started on fetched remote dev ca6f793617 and reconciled onto PR #3644 at e79e5f3945. ADR required: no. Use ADR-003 and ADR-094 for Jobs delivery and owner-scoped domain publication.

An interrupted create-new import used to allocate a second pack. An interrupted update treated its own partial changes as a changed plan. A journal now records its staged local rows before writing bytes. Redelivery reuses their IDs and validates the registration. The generated-file item lock serializes publication and cleanup. Public reads and generic Storage mutations protect the staged rows until the journal commits.

The stack preserves the workbench's original receipt, no-write quota retry, and staged archive removal. Status and cancellation validate the owning Jobs operation and resource. An accepted delivery can revalidate a retained archive after the new-admission deadline. Legacy journals that show writes without an attributable checkpoint require manual recovery.
