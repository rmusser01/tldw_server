## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Phase 5 for Slides and Infographics: prototype PPTX export separately and decide whether fidelity, dependency cost, and maintenance burden justify shipping it.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Prototype exports representative title/content, image-heavy, cited, table, and infographic-layout decks to PPTX.
- [ ] #2 Evaluation records layout/text/image/citation fidelity against the canonical artifact and at least one independent viewer.
- [ ] #3 Evaluation records dependency size, license, supported runtimes, performance, and operational cost.
- [ ] #4 Maintenance risks cover unsupported blocks, font substitution, renderer drift, security updates, and fallback behavior.
- [ ] #5 A written go/no-go recommendation links prototype artifacts, measurements, known gaps, and the minimum shippable support matrix.
<!-- AC:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES:BEGIN -->
Treat PPTX as an investigation, not an implicit shipping commitment. Use a fixed representative corpus and record visual fidelity, dependency/runtime cost, licensing, maintenance risks, and fallback behavior before making the go/no-go recommendation.
