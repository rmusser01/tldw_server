provider_model_inventory's catch-all reports an EgressPolicyError as 'unreachable' and negatively caches it for 20 s, so a policy refusal looks like a provider outage; that disguised TASK-13950.88. A caller with a short deadline can also cache 'unreachable' under a key other callers share. Generation maps the same error through _map_provider_egress_error.
