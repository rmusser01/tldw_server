## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
LLM provider calls now go through one boundary, core/LLM_Calls/gateway.py, which owns perform_chat_api_call(_async), provider/model resolution and API-key resolution, and runs pre/post policy hooks (no-op by default; proven for chat, workflows, RAG and MCP). LLM_Calls no longer imports core.Chat (0 statements, down from 47), importing a provider adapter no longer loads chat_service, and shrink-only ratchets plus an import-isolation guard run in backend-required. ADR-025 and its Published mirror document the gateway and hook contract.
