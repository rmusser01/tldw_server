## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
UAT-082: after Bob completes his own source ingest and normally logs out, admin logs in and opens Quick Ingest. The wizard shows Bob source filename, completed result and source actions despite verified admin authority and an empty admin Media library. Diagnose and repair retained private ingest state and delayed callbacks across logout, account and server changes. No stale action was clicked; cross-account backend access is not alleged.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Current account never sees prior-account ingest filenames, source content, results, progress, or source actions after logout/login or server switch.
- [ ] #2 Delayed ingest polling, completion, cancellation and storage hydration cannot publish or act under a replacement authority, including A to B to A.
- [ ] #3 Same-account close and resume behavior remains usable; original backend jobs are not deleted or cancelled under another account.
- [ ] #4 Behavior regressions, independent review, scoped validation and targeted same-browser multi-user verification pass before the next full UAT.
<!-- AC:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Application behavior is frozen until the current full multi-user matrix finishes. Evidence: /private/tmp/uat-cycle3-multi-bob-admin-ingest-add.txt and /private/tmp/uat-cycle3-multi-bob-admin-authority.txt. Read-only diagnosis pending.

Read-only diagnosis: quick-ingest-session persists a tab-global session without server/principal identity; logout does not clear it and QuickIngestButton resumes terminal results. Wizard also populates unscoped recentlyIngestedDocs consumed by DocumentPicker. Reattach and serial direct batch resolve current credentials, while late tracking/upsert can recreate cleared state. Repair must bind persisted identity and operation authority generation, cover serial dispatch/upload/poll/cancel/result actions, reject legacy unowned records, and preserve same-principal token refresh and same-account resume. Async consequences are source-derived; no stale action or cross-account write was live tested. Design and plan updated.
