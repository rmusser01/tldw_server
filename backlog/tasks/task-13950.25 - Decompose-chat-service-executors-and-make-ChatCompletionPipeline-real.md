Mechanism found by the final review (record): when an asyncio Task resumes a coroutine chain by throwing a failed Future's exception into it, CPython's _gen_throw delegates down the chain without pushing the outer coroutines' exception state. A coroutine moved out of an except body therefore loses the handled exception after such a suspension (a bare raise fails), and its own raises are re-chained at the new coroutine boundary. This explains PR E's N10 __context__ re-chain (N10 stayed inline). The plan's R11 claim that a bare raise in a moved handler span works across suspensions was wrong; the plan now carries a correction and the tool refuses such spans (rule V18, opt-out handler_await).
