## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
PR #2612 uses distinct blobs for each original-file registration to prevent failed or concurrent reuploads from destroying another registered original. Current MediaFiles rows are retained, cleanup protects their paths, and quota recalculation may not scan the configured original storage root. This follow-up must preserve the currently served original under concurrent uploads and partial database or storage failures.
