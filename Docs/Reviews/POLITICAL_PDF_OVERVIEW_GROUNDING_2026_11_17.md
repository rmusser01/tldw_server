## Where information was lost

The overview writer received chapter summaries, their citation IDs, a coverage checklist and scoped author notes. Successive 24,000-character reduction groups were asked for at most 250 words and a few IDs per bullet. The final writer saw the resulting prose and eligible IDs. The strict checker subsequently saw the exact numbered passages. A correct chapter byline or an ID from every chapter could therefore coexist with a missing policy passage.

Three observable handoffs illustrate different causes.

- **Information lost after a correct chapter binding.** Labor chapter finding 6 paired the Sabbath premium-pay proposal with its alternative at E18.1.30 and E18.1.31. Overview finding 20 retained only E18.1.30 and blurred required premium pay into a mandate to work. The Financial chapter attached the cost estimate to E27.1.1. Overview finding 31 attached E27.1.6, which discusses small offerings. Finding 30 had E27.1.1 attached to another proposition.
- **An inherited incomplete binding.** Justice chapter finding 3 already omitted E17.1.13 from a cluster starting at E17.1.12. The extraction grouped E17.1.12, E17.1.13 and E17.1.14 for the same point. Overview finding 19 retained only the list introduction. The operative review recommendation starts at canonical character 1,647,326.
- **Context and evidence serve different purposes.** The Intelligence author note establishes that policy views should be described at chapter level. Finding 8 used appropriate caution but attached a DNI organizational-history passage to a factual explanation of that authorship convention. In findings 17 and 21 the checker also questioned authors whose bylines were established. Those two rows still lacked the policy evidence below.

Job 61 did not retain every successful intermediate reduction response. The chapter-to-overview losses above are directly observable. The saved record cannot identify the individual reduction or final model call that dropped a particular ID.

## Repair and repeatable checks

The production overview writer now receives exact source-validated passages. When a compressed point cites one member of an extracted claim, the writer also receives the explicitly registered passages for that same claim within that portion. Text inside a quote cannot authorize another ID. The writer attaches citations sentence by sentence and narrows details that the available source cannot establish. A small overview fits in its normal final call. A larger overview first drafts across all chapters, then edits groups against their local evidence before the ordinary coverage and Claims checks. Each grounded editor request counts its system prompt, draft, author notes, cited passages and allowed-ID list against 24,000 characters.

A standalone suite of eight paired controls exercises the distinct failure mechanisms through the existing verification-regressions stage. Each pair changes the cited support or the policy wording. All quotes occur exactly once in the same canonical source. The examples cover the FBI recommendation crossing a passage boundary, the misbound public-company cost estimate, premium pay versus compulsory work, and both sides of the EXIM disagreement. The fixture records its agent-label provenance and pending human review. [Fixed control inputs](../../Helper_Scripts/Evals/configs/political_pdf_overview_regressions.v1.json)

The repository eval runner accepts an explicit per-model checkpoint in `summary.resume_artifact_ids` and submits it through the public workspace output endpoint. The worker runs ordinary saved-section synthesis and the ordinary strict Claims verification at the same generation settings. Run the following from the repo root with an owned, qualified workspace and explicit config. Use a fresh run directory for each new intentional generation.
