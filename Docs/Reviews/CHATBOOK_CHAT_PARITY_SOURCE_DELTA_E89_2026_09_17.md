## Terminology and ownership to retain

- **Resume conversation / Restore and resume** selects the original saved conversation and active branch; **Use as source** stages context into the current Console. Existing draft preservation, no automatic send and separate unstaging are existing behaviors clarified by the guide, not newly introduced resume support. The new excerpt makes source use materially richer without changing those roles.
- **Local source**, **active workspace membership**, **model/provider selection**, **sync enrollment**, and **external execution target** remain distinct. The conversation builder explicitly retains local runtime/source ownership. The new RAG local reader refuses server evidence rather than converting it to local identity. Neither implies moving ownership of the current chat.
- **Membership receipt**, **UI handoff acknowledgment**, **configuration file replacement**, **session re-chunk summary**, and **native atomic fork receipt** are different guarantees. Do not promote any of the first four to H2 recovery/deduplication.
- The accepted WebUI/full-page extension parity target, compact sidepanel, independent local ownership and optional sync remain binding. Nothing inspected gives `tldw-agent` chat runtime, conversation storage, sync, browser-shell or model-hosting responsibilities. It remains server-directed external OS/tool execution.
