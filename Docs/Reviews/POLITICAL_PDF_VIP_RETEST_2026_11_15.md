## Evaluation method

Agent-operated walkthrough and code/trace review, using [NN/g's ten usability heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/). Severity follows [NN/g's 0–4 scale](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-to-rate-the-severity-of-usability-problems/), considering frequency, impact and persistence: 0 not a problem, 1 cosmetic, 2 minor, 3 major, 4 release-blocking for the evaluated task. Ratings below are provisional evaluator judgments, not participant measurements or NN/g certification. Independent review, automated checks and actual retest outcomes are recorded separately below.

Heuristic key: H1 system status; H2 familiar language/concepts; H3 control/freedom; H4 consistency; H5 error prevention; H6 recognition over recall; H7 efficiency; H8 minimalism; H9 error diagnosis/recovery; H10 help.
